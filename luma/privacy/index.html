Luma Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026. Publisher: Jefferson Timotius Mansur.

Your wallpapers

Luma imports and processes selected images and videos locally. It stores copies in the app's local library. It does not send your media to a developer-operated server. Removing an item from Luma removes its local copy, not the original files.

Photos and files

File selection uses Apple's system picker. Luma stores selected media in its local app library. Your device backups, iCloud Drive, and other Apple services may sync or back up media according to your settings and Apple's policies. Selected media may retain its original embedded metadata.

Purchases and trial

Apple processes the free trial and permanent unlock through the App Store. Luma checks verified purchase entitlements and dates on your device to determine whether wallpapers need a watermark. Luma does not receive your payment-card information, send your purchase history to a developer server, or automatically charge after the 14-day trial. Contact Apple for App Store payment and refund requests.

Local preferences

Luma saves your library settings and whether the trial offer has been shown on your Mac. These preferences are not sent to the developer.

No tracking or analytics

Luma contains no ads, tracking SDKs, third-party analytics, or developer account system. It does not collect or transmit analytics or your personal media to the developer.

Support

If you contact support, the publisher receives the information you voluntarily include, such as your email and problem description, to answer your request. Avoid including private media unless needed for your request. Email: support.ceastapps@gmail.com. Phone: +62 813-7311-8686.

Changes

Changes to Luma's data practices will be reflected in this policy and the App Store privacy information.
